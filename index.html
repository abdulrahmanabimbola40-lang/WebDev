THE BASICS

What is Web Development?

Web development is the process of creating and maintaining websites and web applications. It combines programming, design, problem solving and other technologies to create useful experiences on the web.

A website can be simple, such as a personal page, or complex, such as an online banking platform or social network.

Why is it important?

Websites help people communicate, learn, shop, work, share information and access services from almost anywhere.

FUTURE OPPORTUNITIES

Careers in Web Development

Front-End Developer

Builds the part of a website that users see and interact with.

Back-End Developer

Works with servers, databases and the systems behind websites.

Full-Stack Developer

Works across both front-end and back-end development.

UI/UX Designer

Focuses on how a website looks, feels and works for its users.
